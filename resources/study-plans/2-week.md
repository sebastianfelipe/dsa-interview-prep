# 2-Week Intensive Plan

**Goal:** Cover the patterns that appear in most interviews.

| Day | Focus | Section | Problems to internalize |

| 1 | Complexity + strategy | [Fundamentals](/reference/topics/00-fundamentals) | — |

| 3 | Prefix sum + hashing | [Arrays & Strings](/browse?topic=01-arrays-and-strings), [Hashing](/browse?topic=02-hashing) | Subarray Sum Equals K, Group Anagrams |
| 4 | Linked lists | [Linked Lists](/browse?topic=03-linked-lists) | Reverse List, Detect Cycle, Merge Two Lists |
| 5 | Stacks | [Stacks & Queues](/browse?topic=04-stacks-and-queues) | Valid Parentheses, Daily Temperatures |

| 7 | Review + timed mock (2 problems) | Key Extracts | — |

## Key Extract

Two weeks is enough for **pattern fluency**, not encyclopedia coverage. Depth on these patterns beats shallow breadth.
